About Dream AI Art

Founded in 2022 by a team of artists and AI researchers, Dream AI Art emerged from a vision to democratize digital art creation. What started as a small experiment in AI-powered creativity has grown into a powerful platform used by thousands of professionals worldwide.

Our mission is to empower creators with cutting-edge AI technology while maintaining the human touch in digital art. We believe in sustainable innovation, ethical AI practices, and fostering a community where creativity knows no bounds. Our core values of accessibility, innovation, and quality drive everything we do.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI art generation process work?

Our AI uses advanced machine learning algorithms to analyze millions of artworks and generate new pieces based on your input. You can specify styles, themes, and other parameters to guide the creation process.

What file formats are supported for export?

We support all major file formats including PNG, JPEG, SVG, and PSD. Premium users can also access layered files and higher resolutions.

Do I own the rights to the generated artwork?

Yes, all artwork generated through our platform belongs to you and includes full commercial usage rights. You're free to use, modify, and sell the generated content.

Is there a limit to how many images I can generate?

Free accounts can generate up to 20 images per day. Premium subscriptions offer unlimited generations and priority processing.

Can I use the service for commercial projects?

Absolutely! Our platform is designed for both personal and commercial use. Many businesses use our service for marketing, product design, and content creation.
